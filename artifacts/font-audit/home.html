A few details

What does Clocked track?

By default, only work sessions based on activity, lock, and idle events. No screenshots or keylogging. Optional project tracking adds focused apps and site hostnames, never full URLs. Team managers can review hours, without live monitoring.

Do I need hosted access?

No. The desktop app keeps your hours locally in SQLite and works without an account. Hosted access adds sync, the dashboard, teams, and scheduled email reports. Approved requests receive an invitation to create an account.

Can I self-host?

Yes. Point the desktop app at your own Cloudflare Worker. The source and setup instructions are on GitHub.
