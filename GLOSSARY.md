# Clocked

## Language

**Session**:
A continuous span of recorded presence, with a clock-in and a clock-out. A session can cross local midnight.

**Timesheet**:
An account's recorded work for a selected calendar month or seven-day period. Each session belongs to local dates in its recorded timezone, with the report timezone as the fallback.

**Worked time**:
Elapsed time covered by at least one session. Overlapping sessions from multiple computers count once.

**Project totals**:
Time attributed to projects through optional activity tracking. These totals are separate from worked time based on presence.

**Timesheet delivery**:
The recipients and monthly or weekly schedule used to send an account's timesheet. A team controls delivery for its members; a solo account controls its own delivery.

**Primary membership**:
The membership that determines an account's workspace, delivery settings, working hours, and permission to adjust timesheets. A team takes precedence over a legacy personal workspace. When an account belongs to several teams, its earliest team membership is primary.

**Manager**:
A person whose membership in a team includes the owner or admin role. Manager authorization applies only to that team.

**Shared timesheet**:
The part of a member's timesheet a team can view, adjust, and send. It includes only sessions that started on or after the member joined that team. Earlier sessions remain in the member's personal history.
